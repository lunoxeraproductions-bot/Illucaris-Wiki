# The Twelve Races

Illucaris is home to twelve peoples, split into two broad groups by one defining trait: whether they carry the **Chimeravore Gene**.

All twelve, regardless of group, share one thing at a biological level — the **Quiet Glow**, a baseline bioluminescence that runs through all native life on Illucaris. It isn't a product of the Gene; it's older and planet-wide, expressed differently by every race, from a scale's flicker to a stone-crack seam of light to a pulse along the bloodstream.

## The Essenoid peoples

The six Essenoid races carry the Chimeravore Gene, letting them absorb essence and traits from living and non-living matter and fuel transformation with it — at the cost of consuming their own body mass as energy. There's no fixed ceiling on what a Chimeravore mutation can become; it scales with how much essence is absorbed and how long the exposure lasts.

- **Elderon** — the longest-lived of the twelve (draft range roughly 550–650 years), with a quality-over-quantity, sacred-life culture. They treat their deepest transformation ritual, Core-Fusion, as rare and sacred rather than routine.
- **Feralith** — a pure-breed ritual culture that deliberately curates pairings toward stable, heritable traits, and originated a multi-core ritual variant of Core-Fusion.
- **Essentari** — a blended people, proud of trending toward a mixed Essenoid/Humanoid identity rather than treating it as a loss of purity.
- **Maerith** — the deliberate "average," control-case race for the Essenoid side, culturally still underdeveloped and open for expansion.
- **Helionth** — healers, whose gift is tied directly to a mortal's split-essence and identity-coherence rather than simple biology.
- **Syntari** — largely opts out of the quantity-vs-quality cultural framing entirely, instead pursuing an essence-purist, technology-forward path: engineering past biology rather than working with it.

## The Humanoid peoples

The six Humanoid races are near-immune to the Chimeravore Gene. Instead of transforming biologically, they lean on **Weaveworking** — engineered, mechanical, and ritual means of doing artificially what an Essenoid's body does natively (see [Magic & Machines](magic-and-machines.md)).

- **Elyrians** — formerly "Humans" in early drafts, now their own distinct people: versatile explorers, traders, and inventors from the Kingdom of Valendar, blending arcane tradition with steampunk craft.
- **Sylvarians** — formerly "Elves," the tallest of the six (6–7 ft) and the brightest, most innate expression of the Quiet Glow among the Humanoid races. Culturally, they lean hard toward "nature over machine."
- **Durkarin** — a clan- and lineage-driven culture, quality-leaning like the Elderon on the Essenoid side.
- **Tinkaryn** — a quantity-leaning, flashy, short-lived Weaveworking style; their Quiet Glow quite literally lives in the formula-marks of their craft.
- **Harrowyn** — sits in contrast to the Tinkaryn: slower, more enduring, with a Quiet Glow that shimmers at dusk and dawn, echoing the rhythm of their crops.
- **Adamaryn** — an open-ended outlier; their lifespan isn't placed on the normal numeric scale at all, with genuine open-ended longevity (given proper maintenance) treated as a live possibility rather than settled fact. Their glow, Runic in character, is tied to the same ancestral Quiet Glow as everyone else's.

## Why the split matters

The Essenoid/Humanoid divide isn't just biology — it shapes culture, politics, and conflict across Illucaris: quantity vs. quality approaches to life and lineage, nature vs. machine philosophies, and old tensions between peoples who transform and peoples who build. It's one of the central tensions the setting is built to explore, on every scale from personal relationships to continent-spanning history.
