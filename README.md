# Illucaris

Illucaris is a world built around a simple, strange fact: some of its people were born able to devour the world and become something new.

Two suns rise over it in staggered shifts. A shadow-touched moon governs the hours when reality's boundaries run thin. Twelve peoples — six who carry a gene that lets them absorb essence and transform, six who build steampunk machines to do artificially what the others do by nature — share one planet, one history, and an uneasy set of overlapping myths about how it all started.

This wiki collects that worldbuilding as it develops — cosmology, races, magic and machinery, history — from **Lunoxera Productions**, the studio building Illucaris into games (starting with a small survival roguelite, working toward something much larger).
