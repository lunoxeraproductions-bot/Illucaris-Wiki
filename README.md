# Illucaris

Illucaris is a world built around a simple, strange fact: some of its people were born able to devour the world and become something new.

Two real suns hold it in a strange kind of daylight — not a main sun and a backup, but two stars locked in orbit around each other, with Illucaris sitting in the one stable pocket of sky between them. There's no true night here. What there is instead: a moon whose shadow can trigger something older and stranger than an ordinary eclipse, leaving pockets of ground that stay wrong long after the sky itself has cleared. Twelve peoples split the difference on how they survive all of it — six who carry a gene that lets them absorb essence and transform, six who build steampunk machines to do artificially what the others do by nature — sharing one planet, one long history, and a set of myths about how it all started that nobody quite agrees on.

This wiki collects that worldbuilding as it develops — cosmology, races, magic and machinery, history — from **Lunoxera Productions**, the studio building Illucaris into games (starting with a small survival roguelite, working toward something much larger).
